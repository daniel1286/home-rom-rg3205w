# Get to know your screen

Home turns the Askey RG3205W into a screen for your home running Android 15: a Home Assistant dashboard, a speaker, a clock or an 8-inch tablet. Here are the basics of the device and of Home.

## The buttons

- **Power**: the white button on the back, next to the power socket.
- **Vol +** and **Vol −**: on top.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Turn the display off or on | Press **Power** once |
| Turn off or restart the device | Press and hold **Power** and choose from the menu |
| Turn the volume up or down | Press **Vol +** or **Vol −** |

## Always plugged in

The screen has no battery: it runs plugged into the mains and turns off if you unplug it. When you plug it back in, it starts up by itself.

It has no clock battery either, so it gets the time from the Internet. That's why it's best to keep it connected to Wi-Fi.

It's made to stay on all the time: by default, the display doesn't turn off when you leave it alone. If you want it to turn off or show the screensaver, you choose that in the setup wizard or later, in [The screensaver](salvapantallas.md).

> [!WARNING]
> With the computer's USB cable connected, the screen doesn't finish starting up. Unplug it before restarting.

## What's inside

| Part | What it has |
| --- | --- |
| Display | 8 inches, 1280 × 800, touch |
| Sound | Two speakers and four microphones |
| Connectivity | 2.4 and 5 GHz Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth |
| Camera | One, turned off by default |

## Home's apps

| App | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Help | This manual, and reporting a problem |
| Updates | Finding and installing new versions of Home |
| Cast | Receiving picture and sound from other devices |
| Screensaver | The time, the weather and the alarm clock when you're not using it |
| Monitor | Seeing how the screen is doing inside, if you installed it |

Help and Monitor can be uninstalled like any other app. You also have the ones you chose in the setup wizard: the Home Assistant dashboard, the house speaker, F-Droid, Aurora Store… On the home screen, swipe up to see them all.
