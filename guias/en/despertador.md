# Alarm clock

The screen can wake you up: the sound starts quietly and gets louder little by little. You set it in the **Screensaver** app, under **Alarm**.

## Setting it

1. Open **Screensaver** and go to **Alarm**.
2. Choose the time.
3. Under **Which days**, tick the days it should ring. With no day ticked, it rings only once.
4. Turn on the switch.

Below it, you can see when it will ring next. On the screensaver, the next alarm shows next to the time.

## How it sounds

- **Volume**: it starts quietly and rises to the level you choose. Let go of the slider to hear a sample.
- **Sound**: by default, Android's alarm sound. Tap **Change** to pick another one.
- **Snooze**: how many minutes it waits if you tap the screen while it rings.

It always rings, whether or not the screensaver is showing: if you have another app in front, it opens on top.

## When it rings

- **Tap the screen** to snooze it.
- Tap **Turn off alarm** to turn it off.

You can also do it from the notification, with **Snooze** or **Turn off**.
