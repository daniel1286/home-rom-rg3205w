# Getting around Home

You use Home with gestures, like today's phones: there are no buttons at the bottom, just a small bar. If you'd rather have the classic three buttons, you can switch in a moment.

## With gestures

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Go back | Swipe from the left or right edge towards the center |
| Go to the home screen | Swipe up from the bar at the bottom |
| See recent apps | Swipe up from the bar, stop halfway up the screen and hold for a moment |
| Switch to the previous app | Swipe left or right along the bar |

In full-screen apps the bar hides. Swipe up from the bottom edge to bring it back and repeat the gesture.

## With buttons

Three buttons show at the bottom, always visible:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Back | Goes back to where you were |
| Home | Goes to the home screen |
| Recents | Shows your open apps so you can switch between them or close them |

## Switching between gestures and buttons

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **System → Gestures → System navigation**. If you can't find it, tap the search icon in Settings and look for “navigation”.
3. Choose **3-button navigation** or **Gesture navigation**.

It changes right away, without restarting.

## Notifications and Quick Settings

- Swipe down from the very top to see your notifications.
- Swipe down again to open Quick Settings: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, brightness and more.
- To close them, swipe up or go back.
