# Your privacy

Home has no carrier apps and nothing that sends your data to anyone. Here's how to control the microphone and camera, and everything that can leave the screen: where it goes and when.

## Microphone and camera

You choose them in the setup wizard; the camera comes turned off. To change it later, go to **Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy controls** and turn **Microphone access** and **Camera access** on or off.

With access turned off, no app can use them, even if it has permission.

## What leaves the screen

| What | Where to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checking for updates | The Home server | When it checks, according to the automatic check, or when you tap **Check now** |
| Downloading an update | The Home server, which counts it and sends you on to GitHub, where it's downloaded from | Only when you download it |
| New version notice in the ticker | The Home server | If you turn it on in Screensaver |
| Help improve Home | The Home server | Once a day, and only if you turn it on |
| Reporting a problem | The Home server | Only when you tap **Send** |
| The weather and the city search | Open-Meteo | With the screensaver |
| The rain radar | RainViewer | With the rain alert on |
| The aircraft | adsb.lol, adsb.fi, adsbdb.com and planespotters.net | With the aircraft alert on |
| The news | The sources you choose | With the headlines or the bulletin on |
| The backgrounds and the map | GitHub, where Home is published | The backgrounds, once when you download the pack; for the map, only the pieces for your area |

Those services only get what they need: the city or area you're looking at, never who you are. None of them needs an account or a key.

## What stays at home

- **Cast** works over your home network. What you send doesn't go through the Internet.
- **Monitor** doesn't use the Internet: what it shows never leaves the screen.
- The **Home Assistant dashboard** only talks to your Home Assistant. Fully Kiosk is downloaded from its website once, when it's installed.

## When it checks for updates

Once a day, when it checks, the screen says which version of Home it has. This is used to count how many screens are on each version. It carries nothing that identifies the screen or you, and the IP address isn't stored.

Everything else in [“Help improve Home”](busqueda-y-ayudar.md) is optional and off by default.
