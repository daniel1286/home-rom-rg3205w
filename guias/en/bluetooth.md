# Bluetooth speaker or headphones

The screen can play through a Bluetooth speaker or headphones, and keyboards and remotes work too.

## Pairing

1. Put the speaker or headphones in pairing mode. This is usually done by pressing and holding their Bluetooth button.
2. On the screen, open **Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device**.
3. Tap the speaker when it shows up in the list and accept.

From then on, it connects by itself whenever it's on and nearby.

## Reconnecting or removing

In **Settings → Connected devices**:

- Tap the device to connect or disconnect it.
- Tap the gear next to it and choose **Forget** to remove it. To use it again, pair it again.

## What works

| What | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Speakers and headphones | Everything on the screen plays through them, in stereo, and you can see their battery level |
| Speaker controls | Pause, next and volume |
| Keyboards and mice | Just like on any tablet |
| Calls | Don't go over Bluetooth: a headset with a microphone only plays sound |

## If it disconnects

- Move the speaker closer to the screen.
- Remove it with **Forget** and pair it again.
- Turn Bluetooth off and on again from Quick Settings.

If nothing changes, [report it](contar-un-problema.md) with the log attached: it helps to see what's going on.
