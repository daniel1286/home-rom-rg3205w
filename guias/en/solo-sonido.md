# Sound only

The screen can work as a speaker for the iPhone, iPad and Mac: it plays whatever you put on, without sending the picture.

## Once, from the app that's playing

In the music, podcast or video app, tap the audio output button and choose your screen. On the Mac you can also use **Sound** in **Control Center**.

While it plays, the screen carries on as usual: nothing takes it over.

## Always, with the “Sound only” option

If you're only going to use the screen as a speaker, turn on **Sound only** in the **Cast** app. Then:

- The iPhone, iPad and Mac see it only as a speaker: they can no longer mirror the picture to it.
- Windows and Android no longer see it: they're only for mirroring the picture.

## The volume

- **Starting volume**, in Cast: how loud it plays when something comes in.
- After that, you turn it up and down from the iPhone or the Mac, or with **Vol +** and **Vol −** on the screen.

To make the screen play in sync with the rest of the speakers at home, see [House speaker](altavoz-de-casa.md).
