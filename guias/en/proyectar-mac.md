# Cast from a Mac

To see what's on your Mac on the screen, or to use the screen as a speaker. The Mac has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the screen.

## Before you start

On the screen, open the **Cast** app and check that it's turned on and that **iPhone, iPad and Mac** is on under **Sources**.

## Mirror the screen

1. On the Mac, open **Control Center**: the icon with two switches, at the top right of the menu bar.
2. Click **Screen Mirroring**.
3. Choose your screen, for example **Living room**.
4. The first time, the screen shows a four-digit code. Enter it on the Mac. After that, it connects by itself.

If you chose **With a password** in Cast, enter the password you set. With **Anyone at home** it doesn't ask for anything.

## Stop mirroring

Open **Screen Mirroring** in **Control Center** again and click your screen to disconnect it.

## Play the sound through the screen

In the Mac's **Control Center**, under **Sound**, choose your screen as the output. That way the sound plays through it even if you're not mirroring the picture. There's more on this in [Sound only](solo-sonido.md).

## If the screen doesn't show up

- Check that the Mac and the screen are on the same network. Guest networks on routers usually don't let devices see each other.
- Go over [Before you start](#before-you-start) again.

> [!WARNING]
> Paid movies and series show up black: their copy protection doesn't allow sending them to another screen.
