# Photos, videos and music

Many photo, video and music apps can send what you have to another screen without mirroring the whole phone or computer. The screen receives it like any other player in your home.

## Before you start

On the screen, open the **Cast** app and check that it's turned on and that **Photos, videos and music** is on under **Sources**.

## From an app

1. In the app, tap the button for sending to another screen or another device.
2. Choose your screen, for example **Living room**.
3. You control it from the app: pause, next, volume…

Photos and videos are shown full screen. Music plays without taking over the screen, so you can keep using it.

Some apps only send to devices of their own brand: those won't see the screen.

## From Windows

1. Right-click a photo, a video or a song.
2. Choose **Cast to Device** and then your screen. In Windows 11 it's under **Show more options**.

To show everything on the computer, see [Cast from Windows](proyectar-windows.md).

## If the screen doesn't show up

- Check that the device and the screen are on the same network. Guest networks on routers usually don't let devices see each other.
- Go over [Before you start](#before-you-start) again.
