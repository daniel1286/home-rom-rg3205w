# Voice

The Voice app turns your voice into text on the screen itself, without the Internet. With it you dictate with the keyboard microphone and talk to apps that understand speech. If you like, it also brings a voice assistant that sets alarms, changes the volume or tells you the time and the weather. It's optional, and what you say never leaves your home.

## Add it

- **In the setup wizard**, in the **Voice** step, turn on **Use voice**. The app is installed and the screen's language is downloaded.
- **If you updated from 2.0**, it's offered in “What's new” for version 2.1, under **Voice without the Internet**.
- **If you didn't tick it, or you removed it**, add it from **Updates → Home apps → Optional**.

When you add it, the screen lets it use the microphone, makes it Android's voice input and turns on its microphone on the keyboard. If the microphone is turned off in **Privacy controls**, voice won't work.

## Dictate

- **In any text field**, tap the keyboard microphone and speak. What you say is typed for you, with its spaces and capital letters.
- **In apps that ask for your voice**, like a voice search, you see “Speak now”.

It stops listening at the end of the sentence, or after 6 seconds without hearing anything. On the voice keyboard you have **Delete**, **Enter** and **Back to the keyboard**.

To try it, open the **Voice** app and tap **Try it**.

## Languages

Each language is downloaded once and then works without the Internet. The app downloads the screen's language and, if you change it, downloads the new one.

| Screen language | What's downloaded |
| --- | --- |
| Spanish, Basque and Galician | The Spanish model, about 40 MB |
| Catalan | The Catalan model, about 43 MB |
| English | The English model, about 41 MB |
| Portuguese | The Portuguese model, about 32 MB |

In the **Voice** app, under **Languages**, you can download another one with **Download** or remove one with **Remove**.

> [!NOTE]
> In Basque and Galician, Voice understands Spanish: you dictate and give commands in Spanish. The answers appear on the screen in your language, and the spoken voice is the Spanish one.

## The voice assistant

You turn it on in the **Voice** step of the setup wizard, with **Voice assistant**, or later in the **Voice** app, on the **Voice assistant** card, with **Use the assistant**.

Tap the **Voice assistant** icon, among your apps, or say your [wake word](#wake-word). A card comes up at the bottom with “Go ahead…” and what it understands as you speak. It answers on the screen and, if you have [Spoken Voice](#spoken-voice), out loud too. The card has **Again** and **Close**, and it closes by itself after a few seconds.

| To | Say, for example |
| --- | --- |
| Set an alarm | “Set an alarm for seven thirty”, “Wake me up at eight” |
| See or cancel your alarms | “Show my alarms”, “Cancel the alarm” |
| Set a timer | “Set a timer for ten minutes”, “Set a timer for an hour and a half” |
| Change the volume | “Turn the volume up”, “Set the volume to fifty”, “Mute”, “Unmute” |
| Change the brightness | “Turn the brightness up”, “Set the brightness to fifty” |
| Ask the time or the day | “What time is it?”, “What day is it?” |
| Ask about the weather | “What's the weather?”, “What's the weather tomorrow?” |
| Open an app or the settings | “Open Cast”, “Open the settings” |

The weather is for the city you chose in the screensaver. If there isn't one, the assistant asks you to choose it.

## Wake word

It lets you call the assistant without touching anything. It comes turned off.

1. In the **Voice** app, under **Voice assistant**, turn on **Wake word**. It shows up when **Use the assistant** is on.
2. Choose one: “Hey Home”, “Hello Home”, “Hey screen” or “Assistant”.
3. Or tap **Write my own**, type it in **Your wake word** and tap **Use**. If it's very short or something people say a lot, it warns you that the screen would wake up by mistake. Another assistant's wake word isn't accepted.

While it's on, the microphone is always listening, without storing or sending anything, and there's a permanent notification: “Listening for "Hey Home"”. Tap the notification to change the wake word or turn it off.

## Spoken Voice

Spoken Voice is another optional app that reads the assistant's answers out loud. It's added if you choose the voice assistant, and you remove it and add it back like the others, from **Updates → Home apps → Optional**.

It uses free voices, one per language: Spanish (also for Basque and Galician), Catalan and English. The Portuguese one will come later; until then, in Portuguese the assistant only answers on the screen. The voice is downloaded once, like the languages, and you change its speed in **Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output**.

## Your privacy

- **When you dictate**, it only listens when you tap a microphone, and stops listening at the end of the sentence.
- **With the wake word**, it's always listening, but it stays on the screen: nothing is stored or sent, and the permanent notification reminds you.
- **Everything happens on the screen**: the audio is never stored and never leaves your home.
- **The Internet** is only needed to download the languages and the voice for Spoken Voice, from Home's address, and for the assistant to tell you the weather.

## Remove it

Voice uninstalls like any app: Android removes its voice keyboard and, the next time the screen starts, it sets Android's voice input back the way it was. To add it back, see [Remove or reinstall apps](quitar-o-poner-apps.md).
