# Wi-Fi, name and time

What you chose in the setup wizard for the network, the screen's name and the time, and how to change it later.

## Changing Wi-Fi network

1. Open **Settings → Network & internet → Internet**.
2. Tap your network and type the password.

You can also do it from Quick Settings: swipe down twice from the top and press and hold **Internet**.

The screen works with 2.4 and 5 GHz networks. If your router has both with different names, the 5 GHz one is better: it's faster for casting.

To set a static IP or change the MAC address, see [Static IP, MAC and certificates](red-y-certificados.md).

## Changing the name

1. Open **Settings → About device → Device name**.
2. Type the new one and tap **OK**.

It's the name the screen shows up with over Bluetooth and when casting, unless you gave it a different one in Cast.

## The time

The screen has no clock battery: it gets the time from the Internet. In **Settings → System → Date & time**:

- **Set time automatically**: leave it on.
- **Time zone**: yours. If you choose it by hand, it still switches to summer time by itself.
- **Use 24-hour format**: on or off, as you prefer.

The screensaver's time format is chosen in its app, under **Screen → Time**.
