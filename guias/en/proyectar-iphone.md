# Cast from iPhone and iPad

To see what's on your iPhone or iPad on the screen, with its sound. They have to be on the same Wi-Fi as the screen.

## Before you start

On the screen, open the **Cast** app and check that it's turned on and that **iPhone, iPad and Mac** is on under **Sources**.

## Mirror the screen

1. On the iPhone or iPad, open **Control Center**: swipe down from the top-right corner. On models with a Home button, swipe up from the bottom edge.
2. Tap **Screen Mirroring**.
3. Choose your screen, for example **Living room**.
4. The first time, the screen shows a four-digit code. Enter it on the iPhone. After that, it connects by itself.

If you chose **With a password** in Cast, enter the password you set. With **Anyone at home** it doesn't ask for anything.

## Stop mirroring

Open **Control Center** again, tap **Screen Mirroring** and then **Stop Mirroring**.

## Just the sound

To listen to music or a podcast through the screen without sending the picture, tap the audio output button in the app that's playing and choose your screen. There's more on this in [Sound only](solo-sonido.md).

## If the screen doesn't show up

- Check that the iPhone and the screen are on the same network. Guest networks on routers usually don't let devices see each other.
- Go over [Before you start](#before-you-start) again.
- If the code doesn't appear, leave **Screen Mirroring** and choose the screen again.

> [!WARNING]
> Paid movies and series show up black: their copy protection doesn't allow sending them to another screen.
