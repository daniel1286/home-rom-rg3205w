# The screensaver

When nobody is using the screen, the screensaver shows the time, the weather and a background that changes through the day. Touch it and you're back to what was there. You choose everything in the **Screensaver** app.

## When it comes on

In **General**, under **When not in use**, choose:

- **Show the screensaver**: it comes on after the time you choose without touching it: 1, 2, 5, 10 or 30 minutes. The screen no longer turns off: the screensaver shows instead.
- **Keep what is on screen**: whatever you have open stays in front, the dashboard or any app. This is the default.

The alarm clock rings either way. Rain, aircraft and the news bulletin only show with the screensaver.

## Your city

The screen doesn't know where it is, so you have to tell it. In **General**, tap **City**, search for your city or town and choose it. That's where the weather, sunrise and sunset, rain and aircraft come from.

## How it looks

In **Screen**:

- **Screen design**: how the time and the weather look. Tap one to try it.
- **Ticker**: a band at the bottom with the weather, headlines and notices.
- **Size**: the clock, the weather and the ticker in **Normal**, **Large** or **Extra large**, to see them from further away.
- **Time**: the system format, 24 h or 12 h, and whether it shows **Seconds**.

## Backgrounds

In **Background**:

- **Automatic** (the default): the background changes by itself depending on the holiday, the weather and the time of day. If you pick one by hand, it stays for an hour and then goes back to automatic.
- **Fireplace**: a fire in the background until you turn it off.
- **Background pack**: free photos and videos for each background. Tap **Download**; it's only downloaded once. Without it, the built-in photo is shown.
- **Holidays and birthdays**: the dates of each holiday and your birthdays. Birthdays with a name show up in the ticker on the day.

## At night

In **Night and brightness**:

- **Night mode**: dark cards, the photo almost off and less brightness. It can follow **With the sun**, **By hours** or **Never**.
- **Daytime brightness** and **Night brightness**. With the sun, the brightness rises and falls gradually at dawn and dusk.

## With the Home Assistant dashboard

If you use Fully Kiosk, turn off its option that keeps the screen on (**Keep Screen On**). Otherwise the screensaver never comes on. It's also best not to use Fully's own screensaver at the same time.
