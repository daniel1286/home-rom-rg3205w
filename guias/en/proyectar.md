# How Cast works

Cast turns the screen into a receiver: you can send it the picture and sound from an iPhone, iPad or Mac, a Windows computer or an Android phone, plus photos, videos and music from apps. Everything goes over your home network, without the Internet.

## Turning it on

Open the **Cast** app and turn on the switch at the top. Under the title it says “Ready to receive as” followed by your screen's name: that's the one you'll choose from your other devices.

It comes turned on, set to **With a code the first time**.

While it's on, when you swipe down from the top you'll see “Ready to receive” at the bottom, among the silent notifications. It doesn't show an icon at the top or make a sound. Tap it to open Cast.

## Sources

Each way of sending has its own switch under **Sources**:

| Option | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone, iPad and Mac | Mirroring their screen and sending their sound |
| Windows and Android | Mirroring the screen of a computer or phone |
| Photos, videos and music | From apps that send to another screen, and from “Cast to Device” on Windows |

If you don't use one of them, turn it off and the screen will stop offering itself that way.

## Who can send

- **With a code the first time**: the first time, Apple devices enter a four-digit code shown on the screen, and other devices ask for permission on it. After that, they connect by themselves.
- **Anyone at home**: any device on your network can send without asking.
- **With a password**: Apple devices use a password you choose; other devices ask for permission on the screen the first time.

The devices you've let in are listed under **Paired devices**. Tap **Forget** on one so it asks again next time.

## Name, picture and sound

- **Screen name**: the one shown when choosing where to cast. By default, the device's name.
- **Quality**: **Like the screen** or **High (1080p)**. The first one is lighter on the Wi-Fi.
- **Sound only**: the screen works as a speaker for the iPhone and the Mac, with no picture. See [Sound only](solo-sonido.md).
- **Starting volume**: how loud it plays when something comes in.
- **Keep the screen on while receiving**: so it doesn't turn off or show the screensaver halfway through.

## While receiving

- What comes in is shown full screen, on top of everything. If the screensaver was on, it goes away.
- It only receives from one source at a time: whatever starts last cuts off the previous one.
- To stop from the screen, swipe down from the top and, in the “Casting from” notification, tap **Stop receiving**.

> [!WARNING]
> Paid movies and series show up black: their copy protection doesn't allow sending them to another screen.

## Removing it or adding it back

If you don't use it, you can remove Cast from the screen.

> [!WARNING]
> Removing it deletes its settings: if you add it back, it starts from scratch, with the name, **Who can send** and the quality set to their defaults and nothing under **Paired devices**. iPhones, iPads and Macs ask for the code again the first time.

1. Open **Updates** and tap **Home apps**, at the bottom.
2. In the **Cast** row, tap **Remove** and confirm with **Remove**.

Its icon goes away and nothing is left running: the screen no longer shows up on your other devices. No update puts it back by itself.

To add it back, open **Home apps** and, under **Optional**, tap **Add it back**. It's instant, with nothing to download.
