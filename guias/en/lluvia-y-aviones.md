# Rain and aircraft

While the screensaver is showing, the screen can let you know when rain is on its way or when an aircraft flies near your home. You choose both alerts in the **Screensaver** app, under **Rain and flights**.

## Before you start

- You need to have chosen your city: in **Screensaver → General → City**.
- The alerts only show with the screensaver in front. If **When not in use** is set to **Keep what is on screen**, they don't show.

## Rain alert

When the radar sees rain near your home, a map comes up showing the rain, where it's heading and how far away it is. It alerts you once per spell of rain.

Turn on **Rain alert** and choose how long it stays on the screen.

## Aircraft alert

When an aircraft flies near your home, its route comes up on the map along with its details: where it's coming from, where it's going, its altitude and its speed, with a photo of the aircraft if there is one. It alerts you about one every 5 minutes at most.

Turn on **Aircraft alert** and choose:

- **Distance**: from 5 to 25 km around your home.
- **Maximum altitude**: 1,500, 3,000 or 6,000 meters, or **No limit**. Low-flying aircraft are the ones you see and hear.
- How long it stays on the screen: from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

## Where the data comes from

The radar comes from RainViewer; the aircraft from adsb.lol and adsb.fi, their routes from adsbdb.com and the photos from planespotters.net, credited to the photographer. You don't need an account or a key. It's all listed under **About** in the app.
