# News and notifications

The screensaver can show headlines in the ticker and, every hour on the hour, a bulletin with news from the sources you choose. Notices can also show up in the ticker. You choose everything in the **Screensaver** app, under **News and notices**.

## The hourly bulletin

Every hour on the hour, while the screensaver is showing, the headlines come up one by one, with their photo and summary.

1. Turn on **News bulletin**.
2. Under **Lasts**, choose how long it stays: 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.

## Which news

- **From where**: international, national or your region (Canary Islands, Galicia, Madrid, Basque Country).
- **About what**: general, sport, technology, business, culture or science.
- **Sources**: the ones that match what you've chosen. Tick or untick the ones you want.
- **Your RSS feeds**: if your favorite news source isn't there, tap **Add** and paste the address of its RSS feed.

The headlines and photos belong to each source and come from its own RSS feed.

## Notices in the ticker

The ticker is the band at the bottom of the screensaver. Besides the weather and the headlines, it can let you know about:

- **New Home version**: when there's an update in the Updates app.
- **Notifications**: those from the apps you choose. Tap **Grant access** the first time.

> [!CAUTION]
> Anyone looking at the screen can see the notifications in the ticker. Only choose apps whose notifications you don't mind showing.
