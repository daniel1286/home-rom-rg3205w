# Remove or reinstall apps

The screen is yours: whatever you don't use, you can remove. Home's optional apps and the ones you chose in the setup wizard uninstall like any other app; Cast is removed from Updates.

## What you can remove

| App | If you remove it |
| --- | --- |
| Help | You lose this manual on the screen and can't report problems from it. The guide is still on the web |
| Monitor | You can no longer see how the screen is doing inside |
| Web panel | You can no longer control the screen from the browser, and the paired browsers are forgotten |
| Cast | The screen no longer shows up on your other devices, and its settings and paired devices are lost |
| Voice | You lose dictation, the voice assistant and its wake word |
| Spoken Voice | The voice assistant only answers on the screen |
| Fully Kiosk, Snapcast, droidVNC-NG | The dashboard, the house speaker or control from the PC stops working |
| Aurora Store, F-Droid | You can't install apps with them, but the apps already installed stay |

The system apps (Updates and Screensaver) can't be removed.

## Remove an app

1. Open **Settings → Apps** and tap **See all apps**.
2. Tap the app.
3. Tap **Uninstall** and accept.

You can also press and hold its icon and drag it to **Uninstall**.

Cast has no **Uninstall**: open **Updates**, tap **Home apps** and, in the **Cast** row, tap **Remove** and confirm.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you remove a Home app (Help, Monitor, Web panel, Voice or Cast), no update puts it back by itself: it only comes back if you add it.

## Reinstall an app

The Home apps (Help, Monitor, Web panel, Voice and Cast) are added from Updates, also the ones you didn't tick when you set up the screen:

1. Open **Updates** and tap **Home apps**, at the bottom.
2. Under **Optional** you see the ones that aren't installed: those you didn't tick say “Optional: not added”, and those you removed say “You removed it: it only comes back if you add it”.
3. Tap **Add** or **Add it back**.

Home installs the version it comes with, or a newer one if there is one. Cast comes back instantly, with nothing to download, and starts from scratch. None of them comes back by itself.

Aurora Store, F-Droid, Fully Kiosk, Snapcast and droidVNC-NG can be installed again from Aurora Store or F-Droid, or from their website.

While you don't have Help, you can read the manual on the web, in the [Home guide](https://github.com/daniel1286/home-rom-rg3205w/blob/main/guias/en/README.md).
