# Report a problem

If something isn't working and it's not in [Common problems](problemas-frecuentes.md), report it from the Help app. It reaches the person who makes Home, and the answer shows up on the screen itself.

## Report it

1. In **Help**, tap **Report a problem**.
2. Under **What is it about?**, choose the area: system, screen, sound, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Home Assistant, screensaver, Cast, updates, setup wizard or something else.
3. Under **What happens**, write it down. The more specific, the better: what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
4. If you like, turn on **Attach the system log**. It helps a lot to find the fault.
5. Tap **Review**.

> [!TIP]
> If you have Monitor, tap **Create report** in its **Overview** and paste the summary into **What happens**: it shows how the screen was doing.

## This is what is sent

Before sending it, you see exactly what leaves the screen: what it's about, the Home version and what you wrote, plus the whole log if you attach it. Email addresses, IP addresses and MAC addresses are removed first. The log also goes without anything that says who you are or where the screen is.

If it's all right, tap **Send**. You get a code, such as `H-7K3Q2M`.

## My reports

**My reports** lists the ones you've sent from this screen:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Received | It has arrived and is waiting |
| Being reviewed | Someone is looking at it |
| Fixed | It's been fixed, and it says in which version of Home |
| Closed | There's nothing more to do with it |

When there's an answer, it shows up underneath. Tap **Refresh** to see if there's anything new.

To remove one, tap **Delete**: it's also deleted from the server and can't be undone.

## What isn't sent

Not your name, not the screen's name, not your network, nor anything that identifies it. Only what you see in “This is what is sent”.
