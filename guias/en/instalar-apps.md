# Install apps

Home has no Google account and no Google store. You install apps with Aurora Store, which downloads apps from Google Play without an account, and with F-Droid, the store of free and open-source apps. Both come ticked in the setup wizard.

## With Aurora Store

1. Open **Aurora Store**.
2. The first time, sign in anonymously: you don't need an account.
3. Search for the app and install it.

That's how you get Chrome, YouTube or Spotify, for example.

## With F-Droid

1. Open **F-Droid**. The first time, it takes a little while to load the list of apps.
2. Search for the app and install it.

All the apps on F-Droid are free and open source.

## Updating apps

Each store lets you know about new versions of the apps you installed with it. Home's own apps are updated along with Home, from the [Updates](actualizar.md) app.

> [!NOTE]
> Some apps need Google services to work fully, for example to receive notifications. Without them, they may not work well.

## If you didn't tick the stores

If you unticked Aurora Store or F-Droid in the setup wizard, they aren't installed. You can download F-Droid from its website, f-droid.org, and install Aurora Store from F-Droid.
